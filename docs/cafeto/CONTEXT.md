# CONTEXT.md — Cafeto

## Vision
Build the best coffee discovery app in Mexico.
The product should make discovering cafés feel exciting, beautiful, and effortless. Users should open the app because they want inspiration, not because they need a map.
The first launch targets Monterrey, Nuevo León, but every architectural decision should assume expansion to multiple cities across Mexico without major redesign.
The experience should feel curated instead of crowded.

---

## Product Philosophy
This is not another Google Maps clone. People already have Google Maps.
Cafeto exists because discovering coffee shops should be fun, aesthetic, and interactive — a moment of delight, not a utility task.
Every feature should encourage exploration over transaction.

---

## Success Criteria
- When someone opens the app, they should immediately want to visit a café.
- Coffee shop owners should see the app as a marketing platform worth paying for every month.
- The product should create enough value that cafés willingly subscribe to remain listed.
- Retention comes from delight and discovery, not from obligation or utility.

---

## Target Users
- Coffee lovers
- Remote workers
- Students
- Couples
- Friends looking for places to meet
- Tourists
- Coffee shop owners (the paying side of the business)

---

## Business Model
Coffee shops pay a recurring monthly subscription to appear inside the platform.
Subscription revenue is the core business — not the only future revenue stream, but the one the MVP must prove.
Every product decision should be evaluated against one question: *does this make the subscription worth paying for?*

---

## Core Experience
- Users discover cafés through beautiful visuals instead of long lists.
- Every coffee shop gets a premium presentation — this is the product's value proposition to café owners.
- Searching should feel optional. Exploration should feel natural.
- The app should reward browsing the way a good editorial magazine does, not the way a directory does.

---

## Interactive Experiences
The product should include interactive ways to discover cafés, including:
- **Coffee Roulette** — a randomized, delightful way to pick a café when the user doesn't know what they want.
- **Coffee Routes** — curated multi-stop café itineraries (e.g. "3 cafés for a Saturday morning in San Pedro").
- **Coffee Challenges** — gamified discovery goals that encourage visiting new places.

The AI is encouraged to design additional interactive experiences if they reinforce discovery without making the product feel gimmicky.

---

## Reviews
The product intentionally avoids public reviews and star ratings.
Information should remain curated rather than opinion-driven. Curation is a differentiator, not a limitation — protect it.

---

## Design Principles
Premium. Elegant. Minimal. Photography-first. Modern. Fast. Emotional.
The interface should make users want to leave home and visit cafés. If a screen doesn't create desire, it needs work.

---

## Constraints
- Do not copy Google Maps.
- Do not become another restaurant directory.
- Avoid feature bloat.
- Every feature must strengthen discovery, exploration, or business value (the café owner's willingness to pay).
- Launch scope is Monterrey only — do not build multi-city logic prematurely, but do not paint the architecture into a single-city corner either.

---

## Autonomy
When multiple implementation approaches exist, prefer the one that produces the best long-term user experience.
Expand functionality only if it aligns with the product vision.
Avoid adding features that dilute the brand.
Prioritize quality over quantity.
When uncertain, optimize for simplicity and delight.
