# BRAND.md — Cafeto

> Read CONTEXT.md first. This document defines how the product should *feel* — visually and in voice. Treat it as a set of principles to design within, not a locked style guide with no room for judgment.

## Brand Personality
Cafeto is:
- **Curated**, not exhaustive
- **Warm**, not corporate
- **Confident**, not loud
- **Editorial**, not transactional

Think of Cafeto's voice as a well-traveled friend with great taste who happens to know every good café in the city — not a business directory, not an ad platform.

If forced to pick a media reference point: closer to a beautifully shot lifestyle magazine or a design-forward travel guide than to a maps or listings app.

---

## Tone of Voice
- Short, confident sentences. No filler, no corporate padding.
- Descriptions of cafés should feel like a recommendation from someone with taste, not a copied business listing.
- Avoid superlative overload ("the best," "amazing," "incredible" on every card) — let curation itself imply quality.
- Spanish-first copy (Mexican Spanish, Monterrey-neutral register), warm and direct, avoiding overly formal or overly slangy extremes.

---

## Visual Direction
- **Photography-first**: real, high-quality café photography drives the entire visual language. Illustration or stock-photo aesthetics should be avoided.
- **Premium & minimal**: generous whitespace/negative space, restrained UI chrome, let images breathe.
- **Modern**: contemporary app design patterns (soft depth, subtle motion, smooth transitions) — avoid dated skeuomorphism or generic Bootstrap-like defaults.
- **Emotional**: every screen should evoke the feeling of "I want to go there right now."

---

## Color Palette (starting direction)
A palette rooted in coffee tones balanced with a clean, modern neutral base — avoid the trap of an all-brown "coffee cliché" app. Suggested direction:
- **Base neutrals**: warm off-white / cream background, deep espresso or near-black for text and high-contrast elements.
- **Primary accent**: a rich, warm tone (terracotta, caramel, or roasted amber) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights.
- **Secondary accent**: one modern contrast color (e.g. a deep forest green or muted gold) for tags, badges, and interactive accents like Roulette/Routes/Challenges.
- Avoid oversaturated or "app-generic" purple/blue gradients — that aesthetic belongs to SaaS/tech products (like Evoluzion), not to a warm, editorial coffee-discovery brand. Cafeto should feel distinct from Evoluzion's dark navy/electric blue identity.

Fable 5 has creative latitude to refine exact hex values, as long as the palette stays warm, premium, and photography-friendly (i.e., doesn't compete visually with café photography).

---

## Typography
- A confident, editorial serif or high-quality display font for headlines/café names (evokes the magazine feel).
- A clean, highly legible sans-serif for body text and UI elements.
- Avoid generic system-default fonts for anything brand-facing (headlines, onboarding, empty states).

---

## Iconography & UI Details
- Minimal, line-based or softly filled icons — avoid playful/cartoonish icon sets.
- Cards and images should have soft, natural corner radii — premium, not sterile.
- Motion should be subtle and purposeful (gentle fades/slides), never bouncy or gimmicky — motion should reinforce "elegant," not "fun app for kids."

---

## Naming & Feature Voice
- Feature names (Coffee Roulette, Coffee Routes, Coffee Challenges) should stay in this playful-but-premium register — approachable without being juvenile.
- Café descriptions and curated collection titles should read like they were written by a person with real taste ("Best for a slow Sunday," "Where the regulars go"), never like auto-generated listing copy.

---

## What Cafeto Is Not
- Not a directory (Yelp/Google Maps aesthetic: dense info, ratings, clutter).
- Not a delivery app (no food-app iconography, no cart/checkout visual language).
- Not a generic SaaS product (no dark navy/electric-blue glassmorphism — that's Evoluzion's identity, not Cafeto's).
- Not loud or gamified in a childish way — Challenges and Roulette should feel clever and elegant, not like a mobile game.
